## Shipping Policy

Our shipping policy ensures reliable delivery across regions. Standard shipping within the country takes 3-7 business days, while express options deliver in 1-3 days for an additional fee. International shipments go through partnered carriers with tracking from dispatch; customers cover any customs duties or import taxes. Orders ship from multiple warehouses to minimize delays, and we provide real-time updates via email and account dashboard. Free shipping applies to orders over $50 domestically; otherwise, rates calculate at checkout based on weight and destination. Backordered items ship separately with no extra charge, and we notify customers of expected timelines. During peak seasons like holidays, processing may extend by 1-2 days—plan accordingly.

## Privacy Policy Overview

We prioritize user privacy by collecting only essential data like name, address, and payment details for order fulfillment. Personal information stays secure through encryption and access controls, never sold to third parties. Cookies track browsing for personalized recommendations but allow opt-out via settings. Data retention follows legal requirements: order history kept for 7 years, marketing preferences indefinitely until deleted. Users access, correct, or erase their data via account portal or support request. Compliance with GDPR and CCPA means explicit consent for cookies and marketing emails, with easy unsubscribe links. Third-party processors like payment gateways adhere to PCI DSS standards.

## Returns and Refunds Guidelines

Returns accepted within 30 days for unused items in original packaging; electronics have a 14-day window. Initiate via account or email with order number and reason—prepaid labels provided for approved cases. Refunds process in 5-10 business days to original payment method, minus shipping costs unless defective. Exchanges available for size/color issues at no extra charge. Damaged or wrong items qualify for full refunds plus return shipping reimbursement. Non-returnable categories include personalized goods, undergarments, and clearance finals-sale items. Inspect packages upon arrival and report issues within 48 hours for fastest resolution.
